SimProbeWhen does structure-aware matching improve Java similarity?

Independent research+

An experimental comparison of normalized-token similarity (C0) and method-aware one-to-one matching (C1), tested against method reordering, class splitting and renaming.

Result: method-aware matching helped on multi-method reorganizations and gave almost no advantage on single-method external fragments. The boundary condition is part of the finding.

Good research finds the boundary.

My software engineering work is not prior AI safety research. What transfers is the method: look for failure modes, keep negative results, and resist conclusions the data can't carry. AI safety is a direction I'm training toward.

Robustness is not discrimination

Invariance to superficial edits can also make unrelated programs look identical. Measure both.

Validation must test the claim

Single-method fragments cannot show whether a method handles multi-method reorganization.

Evidence stays interpretable

A similarity score is not a probability of plagiarism. Consequential decisions need context and human review.

Reproducibility is a feature

Frozen protocols, deterministic sampling, versioned artifacts and documented failures.
